ABOUT

Public debt data made easier to understand

PublicDebt.eu is an independent data project that makes public debt statistics for European Union countries easier to explore, compare and understand.

WHY THE PROJECT EXISTS

Public data without unnecessary complexity

Public debt statistics are publicly available, but they often come from different databases and are not presented in a form designed for quick comparison.

PublicDebt.eu processes and presents these data in a clear and consistent format so that they can be explored by the general public, journalists and people working professionally with public finance.

DATA

Official sources

The project primarily uses harmonised Eurostat data and data published by the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic.

Source data are automatically downloaded, transformed and checked before the final datasets used by the website are produced.

INDEPENDENCE

An independent project

PublicDebt.eu is not an official website of Eurostat, the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic or any other public institution.

It is an independent project based on publicly available data.

USING THE DATA

Citation and reuse

Data and charts from PublicDebt.eu may be cited or reused with attribution and a link to the original page.

When citing individual statistical figures, we also recommend referencing the original data source, particularly Eurostat or the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic.

PublicDebt.eu processes publicly available data from Eurostat and the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic.
